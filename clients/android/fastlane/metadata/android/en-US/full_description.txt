BMChat / BroMoreChat is a free messenger for communication through email systems.

It keeps familiar private chat, shared chat, attachment, and QR invite workflows while using decentralized email/chatmail infrastructure instead of one central service.

First-pass capabilities:

* Communication through existing email and chatmail servers
* Private chats and shared chats with members
* QR invites and second-device setup
* Attachments, media, and voice messages
* End-to-end encryption and contact verification inherited from BMChat
* Open source code and compatibility with internet standards

BMChat is a fork of BMChat and preserves required upstream attribution.
